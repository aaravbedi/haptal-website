The rule

If the kernel didn't measure it, Anvil doesn't say it.

Every number on the sheet comes from the geometry engine. The AI writes around those numbers and is checked against them. A sentence that can't be verified doesn't ship.

01

Measured, not read off a picture

Faces, holes, walls and fits come from the solid model.

02

Standards, not vibes

ISO 2768, ISO 286, ASME Y14.5. Cited where they apply.

03

You stay in charge

Stated tolerances are kept. Suggested ones are marked draft.

04

It's your drawing

DXF out. Open it, edit it, release it.
